Clip-On Library Shelf Divider

A local elementary school librarian needed a lot of these. Store-bought ones run about $4.50 each, so I modeled one from photos and caliper measurements. A springy hook clips onto the shelf edge, and a ledge along the bottom holds a label. It costs about $1 of PETG to print.

About

I'm a Staff Software Engineer in Eugene, Oregon, and I've been writing software professionally since 2001. Most of that has been backend work: APIs, databases, and keeping systems fast as they grow. I also hold a US patent from my time at Unisys.

These days my work is mostly AI. At Aurora Solar I built Sunny, an AI assistant the whole company uses in Slack, and an MCP server that connects Claude to our internal tools. I like building the pieces that make other engineers faster, and I'm drawn to work that matters, like clean energy and education.

I first saw a 3D printer at SIGGRAPH in my late teens and waited more than a decade to own one. When I found OpenSCAD, I could use programming to make real objects, and I've been designing printable things ever since. My most recent one is a shelf divider for a school library.
